About Joshi Freight Carriers

A Serious Approach to Commercial Road Transportation.

Based in Dhule, Maharashtra, Joshi Freight Carriers provides reliable road freight and cargo coordination services tailored for manufacturers, industrial processors, agricultural traders, and commercial distributors.

Dhule sits at one of the nation’s most important highway crossroads—where the North–South corridor of National Highway 52 (Agra–Mumbai) meets the West–East artery of National Highway 53 (Surat–Nagpur). Operating directly from our office on the Agra–Mumbai Highway beside Hotel Residency Park, right at the Surat Bypass intersection, we maintain continuous, hands-on control over regional vehicle dispatch and highway movement.

With roots grounded in the Central and Western freight belt—originating from Sendhwa, expanding through Indore, and firmly established in Dhule—our operations are built on straightforward commercial relationships: direct telephone access to decision makers, disciplined loading protocols, and prompt physical documentation.

Receive Freight Rates & Plan

03
